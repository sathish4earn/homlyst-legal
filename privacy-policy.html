2. Family Steps and Health Connect

Family Steps is optional and off until you turn it on. On Android, HomLyst reads data from Health Connect only after you grant permission in Health Connect's own screen.

  • What we read: only your daily step count (the Health Connect "Steps" data type, read access). We do not read any other health or fitness data, and we never write data to Health Connect.
  • Your choice: nothing is shared until you switch on "Share my daily steps" for a Family Space and confirm. You choose which Family Space(s) can see your steps, and you can turn sharing off at any time.
  • Who can see it: only the members of the Family Space(s) you chose. Your steps are never public.
  • What we store: your daily step totals, on our server, only while you share with at least one Family Space, so your family can see them. When you stop sharing everywhere, your stored step data is deleted. Deleting your data in the app (Settings → "Delete my data") also deletes it. You can remove HomLyst's access at any time in your phone's Health Connect settings.
  • What we never do: we never sell your health data, share it with advertisers, data brokers or other third parties, use it for advertising, or use it for credit, insurance, employment or lending decisions.
  • HomLyst's use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How your phone number is protected

If you enter your phone number (Settings → Profile) or send a list to someone else's number, that number is sent to our server once, over an encrypted connection. The server immediately converts it into a one-way scrambled value (a keyed cryptographic hash) before saving anything — the readable phone number itself is never written to our database and never appears in our logs.

This means:

  • If our database were ever breached, the attacker would see meaningless scrambled values, not phone numbers.
  • We can still recognize "this is the same phone number as before" (so sharing works), without ever storing it in readable form.
  • We cannot look up or recover your phone number from what's stored — the process only works one way.

This is a real technical safeguard (a form of pseudonymization), but your phone number is still legally "personal data" as long as it's collected at all — this notice, your consent, and your rights below still apply.

5. How long we keep it

  • Shared-list invitations: kept for 48 hours (their normal lifetime), then automatically deleted about a week later at the latest.
  • Device registration (push token, scrambled phone, display name): automatically deleted if the app hasn't been opened in 12 months. Reopening the app afterward simply re-registers it.
  • Daily steps (Family Steps): kept only while you share them with at least one Family Space; deleted when you stop sharing or delete your data.
  • Mood (Family Mood): automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Shopping list content: stays on your device under your control (History screen) until you delete it yourself; we don't have a copy unless you actively share it.

6. Your rights

  • Access — ask what we hold about your device/phone/name (contact us; no automated export tool yet).
  • Correction — change your name/phone/language any time in Settings.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your device's server-side record at any time via Settings → "Delete my data" in the app, or by contacting us directly. This erases your name, phone number, push registration, shared daily steps and moods from our servers, and clears your cart and history on that device; content you've already shared in a Family Space, Event, or Trip Chat may still be visible to other members there.
  • Withdraw consent — simply don't fill in a phone number/name if you don't want to use the sharing feature; the rest of the app works fully offline without it.

7. Children's privacy

HomLyst is not directed at children and we don't knowingly collect data from users under 13 (or the minimum age in your country, if higher). "Family Space" is a household-coordination feature for the adults managing a shared shopping list, not a product for children to use directly.
